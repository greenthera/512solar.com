About 512 Solar

Austin's Premier Solar Contractor

512 Solar is a leading solar panel design and installation contractor, serving businesses and homeowners throughout Texas.

512 Solar is an Austin-based residential and commercial solar design and installation company that creates beautiful, enduring solar power installations for discerning homeowners. Our sleek, high-grade, cutting-edge materials complement the aesthetic of your home and are designed and installed with care by our owner-led team of well-trained, vetted solar experts. The resulting installations add value to your home and business, and because they are entirely upgradable, you can continue to build value with the highest levels of effectiveness and efficiency available.

512 Solar delivers an exceptional customer service experience while navigating your path to energy independence. From our initial consultation through project completion, you will have a single, committed point of contact that will partner with you to optimize the experience every step of the way.

What We Offer

Our Services

From residential solar installations to commercial arrays, EV charging stations, and full roofing services — we offer comprehensive solutions across Texas.

☀️

Solar Panel Installation – Residential

We are residential grid-tied solar photovoltaic systems installation specialists. We design your system elegantly and lead you through a turn-key installation process. Our products are coupled with our premium installation and maintenance partnership to ensure the project's full ROI is realized.

🏢

Commercial Solar Panel Services

We design your commercial solar panel systems elegantly and professionally lead you through a turn-key installation process. Our products are coupled with our premium installation and maintenance partnership to ensure the project's full ROI is realized in the most effective way.

🔧

Solar Service & Maintenance

We help maintain and optimize existing solar systems for our clients. We specialize in Enphase, Solaredge, SMA, PowerOne, and Fronius inverter replacements, keeping your solar investment performing at peak efficiency year after year.

🔍

Roof Inspection

Is your home experiencing roof leaks? Did your roof suffer storm damage? Do you need a roof inspection before buying or selling your home? We can assess your roof, advise on repair or replacement, and provide you with a quote upon inspection.

🏗️

Roof Installation & Repair

We are specialists in residential roof installations and replacements using high-grade composition shingles and standing seam metal roofs, delivering durable, beautiful results that protect your home for decades.

⚡

EV Charging Station Installation

Our owner-led team of well-trained, vetted industry experts will design and install EV Charging Stations to complement the aesthetics of your home while complying with industry standards and electrical code.
